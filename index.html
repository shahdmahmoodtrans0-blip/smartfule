A balanced plate

A practical pattern, not a rigid rule. Combine vegetables, protein sources, carbohydrate sources and fats in proportions that fit you and your culture.

  • Half vegetables & fruit — for fibre, vitamins and volume.
  • A quarter protein — legumes, fish, poultry, eggs, dairy or tofu.
  • A quarter carbohydrates — grains, potatoes, rice, bread, ideally whole.
  • Fats in moderation — olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado.
